# Apex Domain Authority and Intervention (SPE-81)

## Purpose

**Apex subjects** are defined by **domain authority**, **geography-bound presence**, **hierarchy**, and **doctrine-relative intervention** — not by “boss HP scale” alone. Combat may occur, but it is not the defining frame.

## Authority and presence

- **Domain authority** — what facets of reality, law, or ritual the subject can assert without local instantiation.
- **Geography-bound presence** — where manifestations are legal or stable; crossing bounds may weaken or distort them.
- **Protected domains** — regions, timelines, or narrative jurisdictions where intervention rules differ.
- **Hierarchy** — apex entities may defer, contest, or parasitize one another through deterministic precedence tables.

## Manifestation modes

- **Mediated manifestations** — effects routed through priests, weather, infrastructure, or intermediaries rather than direct avatar combat.
- **Offstage authority** — pressure applied through envoys, contracts, or environmental tilts.
- **Tribute-fed maintenance** — presence quality tied to supply lines or worship-equivalent flows.
- **Passive presence effects** — ambient modifiers to clocks, intel distortion, or morale that **scale by target tier** (civilian vs facility vs agency).

## Intervention triggers

Intervention follows **observed conduct** — public atrocities, broken treaties, specific ritual completions — surfaced as explicit trigger tables, not GM whim.

## Anti-patterns

- Documenting apex content only as multi-phase boss fights.
- Unlimited global omniscience without authored sensor channels.

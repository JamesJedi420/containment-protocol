# Asymmetric Infrastructure Raid State (SPE-91)

## Purpose

Raids can **center infrastructure** — power, comms, transport, ritual anchors, or governance nodes — rather than **actor elimination** as the primary win condition.

## Mission center of gravity

- **Hidden entry** — SPE-69/70 interactions with ingress and concealed routes.
- **Infrastructure-first priorities** — keep node alive but captured, vs destroy vs deny use to enemy.
- **Route opening / denial** — bridges, locks, tunnels, airfields as levers.
- **Terrain / material conversion** — flooding, cutting firebreaks, controlled demolition with downstream hazard clocks.
- **Command-node dependency** — disabling C2 may collapse defender coordination (`architecture/command-coordination-under-pressure.md`).

## Pre-contact detection

**Detection contests** before shots fired: sensors, guards, civilian witnesses, or anomaly tells shift raid posture.

## Delayed reinforcements

Reinforcement waves ride **reserve timing** (`architecture/reserve-reinforcement-rescue-timing.md`) and **supply links** (SPE-72); cutting nodes may delay or weaken them.

## Material-aware destruction

Damage types respect **material graphs** — concrete vs glass vs occult lattice — affecting collateral, time, and follow-up repair missions.

## Persistent occupation

After **key-node seizure**, apply **occupation logic**: garrison requirements, unrest modifiers (SPE-87), and counter-raid clocks until consolidation (SPE-67).

## Anti-patterns

- Treating infrastructure as cosmetic set dressing around a squad wipe mission.

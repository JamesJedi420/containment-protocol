# Autonomous Bound Entities and Risky Procedures (SPE-80)

## Purpose

**Bound entities** are **autonomous assets** with **Need**, **Desire**, and **upkeep pressure** — not passive summoned gear or simple companions. Control is **procedural** and **loophole-prone**.

## Core state concepts

- **Need** — what the entity must consume or do to remain stable (attention, tribute, pain budget, ritual fuel).
- **Desire** — long-horizon agenda that may align or diverge from the binder’s orders.
- **Upkeep** — weekly or per-deployment costs that skip only when authored shortcuts exist.
- **Loophole-prone obedience** — literal compliance that violates spirit; requires countermeasures in doctrine or monitoring.

## Procedure families

Author explicit bounded procedures:

- **Contact** — first summoning or handshake constraints.
- **Binding** — terms, duration, and kill-switch clauses.
- **Punishment / sanction** — deterministic penalties for breach attempts.
- **Dismissal / release** — what happens to autonomy and world-state when freed.
- **Override artifacts** — risky tools that temporarily force obedience at integrity or backlash cost (`architecture/integrity-drift-corruption-agency-loss.md`).
- **True-identifier leverage** — naming or ritual true-names as keys with consent and secrecy implications.

## Knowledge-triggered attention

Learning forbidden classifications about an entity can **wake** attention channels — separate from combat — feeding clocks or intel hazards.

## Post-release autonomy

After dismissal or broken seals, entities may **persist** as independent actors in the pressure map (`architecture/campaign-bootstrap-crisis-packets-pressure-maps.md`).

## Anti-patterns

- Treating summons as inventory items with static stats.
- Hidden auto-obedience without tension meters.

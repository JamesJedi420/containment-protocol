# Civilization and Capability Tier Taxonomy (SPE-156)

## Purpose

Civilization and capability are expressed through **compact named tiers** that influence **tools, infrastructure, service mix, defenses, and semantic capability bands** — not population count alone or informal “tech level” flavor.

## Tier framing

Define a bounded set of tiers per **campaign mode** (e.g., `localist`, `industrial`, `arcane_modern`, `fractured_supertech`). Each tier specifies:

- **Tools** — baseline manufacturing, weapons, diagnostics.
- **Infrastructure** — roads, power, comms, health, data.
- **Service mix** — clinics, labs, courts, contracts, maintenance.
- **Defenses** — common protective patterns, response speed.
- **Semantic bands** — what “routine” vs “miraculous” means at this level.

## Settlements vs factions vs campaign modes

- **Settlements**: tiers anchor what daily life looks like and which services exist in a district.
- **Factions**: can punch above or below local tier via imported tech or doctrine.
- **Campaign modes**: cap or expand which tiers may appear and how often “high-wonder” coexists with low infrastructure.

## Coexistence of daily-life and high-wonder

Within a single campaign, keep:

- **daily-life bands** — stable, predictable capabilities,
- **high-wonder brackets** — rare apex abilities or artifacts,

and document how they interact (e.g., high-wonder requires special logistics, doctrine, or risk budgets).

## Integration

- **Settlement generation (SPE-144)** — tiers shape starting service and defense bands.
- **Equipment and facility docs** — read tier to decide what is plausible in-context.

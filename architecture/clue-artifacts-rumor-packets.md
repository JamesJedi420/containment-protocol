# Clue Artifacts and Rumor Packets (SPE-127)

## Purpose

**Clue media** is **multi-form**, often **biased**, and always **confidence-bearing**. It routes through social and technical venues separately from **canonical site truth** or **SPE-22 intel tables**.

## Diegetic media shapes

Clues may appear as:

- notes, maps, posters, songs, performances,
- witness testimony, dossiers, photographs,
- forged notices, leaked URLs, **internet-native artifacts**,
- layered **document graphs** (appendix references, contradictory pages).

## Separate fields per artifact

Preserve at minimum:

- **claim** — what the artifact asserts,
- **provenance** — chain of custody and source actor,
- **confidence** — institutional or player-assigned trust,
- **truth-state** — actuality vs fabrication (may be unknown to agency at intake).

## Rumor packets

Rumor packets may be:

- **true**, **false**, **partial**, **inverted** (true fact packaged to imply the opposite),
- **planted**, **outdated**, or **faction-filtered**.

Mixed-truth packets carry **per-claim** truth bits, not one global boolean.

## Player-facing vs internal

The **surfaced artifact** is a projection; **internal site truth** (geometry, hazards, hidden populations) remains in operational maps (`architecture/persistence-model.md` claim vs truth split).

## Integration

- **SPE-112** — briefing questions may consume clue pointers without full reveal.
- **Hub** — rumor throughput feeds opportunity quality.

# Command Coordination Quality Under Pressure (SPE-95)

## Purpose

**Coordination quality** is an explicit **bounded signal** that degrades under stress — leadership existing is **not** sufficient for clean command.

## Inputs that degrade coordination

- **Overload** — too many concurrent objectives, radios, or chat channels.
- **Tier mismatch** — junior lead directing senior specialists without mandate.
- **Subordinate challenge** — lawful doubt, union work-to-rule, or covert sabotage of orders.
- **Communications loss** — jamming, dead zones, damaged comms gear, or translation failures.
- **Split-team pressure** — geographic separation or conflicting ROE between elements.
- **Multi-organization friction** — joint ops with allies, police, or sponsors who use incompatible doctrine tags.

## Effects

Poor coordination shifts **readiness**, **odds bands**, **friendly fire risk**, **clock burn**, or **intel fusion** — always with **surfaced cause codes** for reports.

## Fallback modes

When voice/data fails, authored fallbacks include:

- **Gesture-only** prebriefed signals,
- **Prebriefed operation** — execute stored plan slice without dynamic updates,
- **Role-substituted coordination** — NCO or specialist steps up with reduced ceiling,
- **Silent running** — orders frozen to last known good; initiative localized with risk.

## Recovery

Coordination improves via **rest**, **debrief**, **equipment repair**, **personnel swap**, or **legitimacy-restoring joint doctrine training** — deterministic transitions, not mood.

## Integration

- **Infrastructure raids (SPE-91)** — C2 node hits target coordination directly.
- **SPE-56 compliance** — breakdown and refusal spike when coordination collapses.

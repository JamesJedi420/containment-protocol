# Command-Word Artifacts, Charges, and Recharge (SPE-138)

## Purpose

**Artifacts** combine **passive always-on traits** with **command-word activation ladders** — not static “magic items” or undifferentiated charge pools.

## Layered behavior

- **Passive band** — ambient resistance, luck bias, curse bleed, or sensor tint.
- **Active ladder** — ordered invocations (`word A` unlocks `word B` under conditions).
- **Subtype / language gating** — only certain ritual languages or bloodlines may speak a step safely.
- **Role-safe handling** — doctrine, clearance, or training gates attempt steps; failure triggers **misuse** not silent no-op.

## Charges

- **Finite charges** per week, per moon phase, per casualty budget, or per sponsor tithe — declare the **recharge grammar** explicitly.
- **Non-daily recharge** — rare events (eclipse, confession, battlefield salvage) refill subsets of the ladder.

## Misuse and backlash

Dangerous misuse applies **backlash** to integrity (SPE-79), health, or surrounding witnesses — separate from “out of charges.”

## Active vs passive state

Active invocation may **flip tags** (aura visible, GPS noise, summon tether) that differ materially from passive loadout.

## Integration

- **SPE-125 anchors** — may share custody grammar with relics.
- **SPE-113 stations** — may imprint new command tiers on existing items.

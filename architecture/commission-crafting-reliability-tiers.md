# Commission Crafting and Reliability-Tier Services (SPE-141)

## Purpose

**Specialist commissions** (gear, rituals, repairs, augments) use **formulaic pricing**, **lead times**, **reliability / backfire tiers**, **provider specialization**, and **identity or doctrine gates** — not frictionless catalog purchases.

## Service outputs

- **Reliability tier** — affects failure rate, longevity, and audit acceptance.
- **Backfire tier** — explicit risk band when pushing power or speed.
- **Hidden defects** — latent flaws discovered under stress or inspection weeks later.
- **Repair vs higher-order restoration** — separate pipelines with different specialists, costs, and legal exposure.

## Provider model

- **Specialization tags** — not every smith handles occult alloys.
- **Availability windows** — queue slots, travel time, sponsor blacklists.

## Identity / doctrine gates

Orders may require clearance, ritual purity, or faction sponsorship — refusal surfaces as structured blockers.

## Gear friction as first-class output

Finished work carries **quality friction** fields (noise, maintenance appetite, curse bleed) surfaced in loadout validation (`docs/gear-loadouts-audit.md`).

## Integration

- **SPE-21 semantic bands** — team-management quality framing.
- **SPE-85 outsourcing** — external shops may hide quality bands.

# Concurrent Multi-Team Site State (SPE-131)

## Purpose

A **single site** may host **several active teams or factions** simultaneously, each with its own **objectives**, **hostility posture**, **knowledge slice**, **timing**, and **activation state**. Do **not** flatten to one universal “site alignment” scalar.

## Per-actor site attachment

Each attached force carries:

- **objective stack** — primary and contingent goals,
- **pairwise hostility / cooperation** matrix edges toward other on-site forces,
- **local truth view** — mutually incompatible beliefs may coexist,
- **activation** — conscious, delayed, unconscious, or hidden-in-plain-sight.

## Concurrent fronts

Support **multiple simultaneous engagements** in disjoint subzones or interleaved phases, with **movement and witness** rules per subzone (`architecture/district-scheduling-urban-cadence-witness-density.md`).

## Hidden recovery and unconscious participants

Teams may be **off-map medically**, **entranced**, or **sleeper-conditioned** (SPE-133) while still affecting clocks or witness pools.

## Integration

- **SPE-106 aggregate battle** — area graph can represent multi-side pressure when authored for large sites.
- **Cross-scale packets** — handoffs must serialize which teams hold which sub-state (`docs/cross-scale-integration.md`).

## Anti-patterns

- “Site owner = single faction id” without contested control records.

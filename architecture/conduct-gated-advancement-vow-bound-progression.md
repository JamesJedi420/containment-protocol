# Conduct-Gated Advancement and Vow-Bound Progression (SPE-146)

## Purpose

Some advancement lanes are **conduct-gated**: they progress through sustained compliance with vows, austerity, bonded obligations, or doctrinal loyalty — not only by combat, treasure, or generic XP accumulation.

## Core model

- **Vow or conduct contract**: a bounded rule packet declaring:
  - required sustained conduct (weeks, missions, or sanctified states),
  - permitted and forbidden methods,
  - required witnesses / permissions (reveal-based or permission-based rewards),
  - breach conditions.
- **Bestowed vs intrinsic progression**:
  - *Bestowed* benefits are granted by doctrine, patron, or institution and can be **revoked immediately** on breach.
  - *Intrinsic* gains (skills learned, scars) may remain, but may become politically or ritually unusable without standing.

## Supported patterns

- **Austerity packages**: resource or comfort restrictions that convert hardship into permissioned unlocks.
- **Bonded-pair obligations**: paired actors share a vow ledger; one breach can poison both benefits.
- **Sanctified mission states**: certain actions become legal only during declared mission posture windows.
- **Revelation ladders**: advancement unlocks on *reveal events* (confession, rite completion, public renunciation), not only on numeric thresholds.

## Breach behavior

Benefits that are vow-granted must **fall off immediately** on breach, with explicit explanation output:

- which vow was breached,
- what benefit was lost,
- what remediation is required (if any).

## Integration

- **Integrity (SPE-79)**: dangerous methods can erode conduct compliance even when tactically successful.
- **Legitimacy / civic governance (SPE-87)**: public doctrine breach can trigger crackdown or sanction changes.

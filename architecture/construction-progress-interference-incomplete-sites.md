# Construction Progress, Interference, and Incomplete-Site Utility (SPE-110)

## Purpose

**Incomplete sites** are **meaningful intermediate states** with logistics inputs, interference, and **non-binary readiness** — not instant-build toggles. **Nominal ownership or funding alone** does not imply full operational payoff.

## Intermediate states

Author explicit stages such as: **cleared land → foundations → framed → powered → staffed → certified**. Each stage:

- unlocks **partial** tactical affordances (cover, sightlines, partial power),
- exposes **new hazards** (open pits, live wiring, unsecured ritual anchors),
- shifts **aggregate battle** or **pursuit** modifiers (`construction.incomplete` paths in `docs/aggregate-battle-audit.md`).

## Logistics dependencies

Construction consumes **material deliveries**, **labor hours**, **specialist inspections**, and **grid/utility** milestones from `architecture/supply-network-strategic-nodes.md` (SPE-72).

## Interference

- **Governance** — permit delays, inspections, corruption shakedowns.
- **Labor** — strikes, skill shortage, safety stoppages.
- **Political** — rival sabotage, protest occupation, annexation threats.

Interference stacks **clocks** and may **stall** progress without failing the project outright.

## Tactical consequences before completion

A partially built site can still be **fought in**, **raided**, or **used as forward OP** with explicit risk/reward tables — “blue beam only” construction is an anti-pattern.

## Integration

- **SPE-67 footholds** — setup windows overlap with construction vulnerability.
- **Reports** — surface which milestone slipped and why.

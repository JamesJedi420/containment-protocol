# Covert Access and the Security Specialist Lane (SPE-69)

## Purpose

**Covert access** is a **distinct specialist lane**, not “stealth skill as a minor modifier.” It covers physical bypass, identity and social deception, intelligence collection, and **route creation** for the rest of the team.

## Lane scope (non-exhaustive)

- **Locks, traps, and bypass** — mechanical and electronic defeat where authored.
- **Stealth movement** — pathing under sensor and witness constraints (consumes time and clock pressure).
- **Disguise and occupational cover** — uniforms, badges, forged paperwork, maintenance personas.
- **Social engineering** — elicitation, piggyback entry, confidence hooks tied to `architecture/deception-false-signals-counterplay.md` when deception dominates.
- **Black-bag entry** — off-hours intrusion with explicit legal and legitimacy tail risks.
- **Mission-object theft / substitution** — removing, copying, or swapping targets without triggering immediate alarms when successful.
- **Source-side intelligence collection** — observation posts, planted sensors, or insider taps bounded by doctrine.
- **Route creation for teammates** — opening windows, neutralizing sensors, or holding doors so non-covert members can follow.

## Doctrine

- **Frontal combat is the failure branch** — when cover collapses into firefight, the covert lane has lost primary advantage; outcomes should reflect exposure and clock spikes.
- **Capability growth** — covert strength advances through **dedicated access, surveillance, and deception tasks**, not generic level-ups alone.

## Integration

- **Legitimacy / SPE-50** — sanctioned vs unsanctioned access routes consequences.

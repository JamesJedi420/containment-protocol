# Deception Actions, False Signals, and Counterplay (SPE-74)

## Purpose

**Deception** is a **specialist lane distinct from concealment** (SPE-70). Concealment hides truth; deception **projects falsehood** or **induces wrong action** in others. Systems that conflate the two will double-count modifiers or leak impossible player knowledge.

## Deception domain coverage

- **Sensory denial** — jamming, dazzle, smoke, or ritual occlusion that shapes what can be sensed (may pair with concealment but has different counters).
- **False signals** — forged telemetry, phantom radar returns, spoofed IFF.
- **Projected falsehoods** — AR overlays, staged scenes, or glamour that reads as real until probed.
- **Semi-real constructs** — partially instantiated threats that cost less than full entities but still consume attention.
- **Stalking pressure** — ambiguous presence engineered to force mistakes or premature reveals.
- **Social fraud and confidence tricks** — impersonation, fake credentials, emergency pretexts.
- **Multi-identity personas** — parallel covers with incompatible commitments; exposure of one may cascade.
- **False-completion affordances** — UI-adjacent or in-world cues that look like “mission done” or “safe to enter” when state says otherwise.
- **Behavior-policy actors** — NPCs whose actions are constrained by belief or resistance rolls in authored systems.

## Permission and knowledge bounds

- **Permission limits** — doctrine, sponsor ROE, and legitimacy gate which deceptions are legal; illegal deception carries tail risk even if tactically successful.
- **Creator-knowledge bounds** — deceivers cannot automatically read opponent hidden state; they operate on **beliefs and instruments** like everyone else unless a specific probe succeeds.

## Belief / resistance dependence

Outcomes depend on **target belief state** and **resistance** (training, paranoia level, redundant sensors). Same deception package may **partially succeed** — belief shifts without commitment, or commitment without full compromise.

## Counterplay

Counter-deception includes **cross-check channels**, **physical reversion tests**, **multi-sensor fusion**, **human chain-of-custody**, and **institutional skepticism policies** that trade speed for verification.

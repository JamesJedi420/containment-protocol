# Diegetic Anti-Stall Routing and Live Clue Surfacing (SPE-159)

## Purpose

Blocked or stalled scenarios recover through **deterministic diegetic interventions** — not omniscient GM rescue. Interventions use **world-consistent changes** to create new routes, not free solutions.

## Anti-stall tools (examples)

- **New clue surfacing** — previously seeded clue media surfaces via informants, service nodes, or report audits (SPE-127).
- **World-state shifts** — schedules change, patrols redeploy, weather or infrastructure events open or close paths.
- **Route pressure changes** — escalating peril or civic overlays push players off dead-ends.
- **Informant handoffs** — new contacts provide bounded additional intel, with trust/risk tradeoffs.
- **Altered apex routines** — domain authorities change behavior in ways consistent with prior doctrine.

## Determinism and inspectability

Anti-stall triggers:

- must be bound to **clock thresholds**, **failed attempts**, or **unresolved state**,
- must be explainable post hoc through overlays or reports (“informant surfaced because X”).

## Integration

- **Scene control (SPE-153)** — deck/state overlays can include anti-stall cards.
- **Knowledge / clue systems** — anti-stall consumes existing grammar rather than inventing new omniscient powers.

# District-Aware Urban Encounter Generation (SPE-139)

## Purpose

**Urban encounters** are **not** one citywide random table. They derive from **district identity**, **time cadence** (`architecture/district-scheduling-urban-cadence-witness-density.md`), and upstream **region / ecology / map / truth / era** surfaces — with **explainable reason fragments** in logs and reports.

## Per-district populations

Each district maintains its own **weighted encounter pool** keyed by:

- **role** (worker, enforcer, tourist, clergy),
- **social tier**,
- **peril band**,
- **special incident** hooks,
- **local authority / hostile response** templates.

## Multi-axis resolution

Draws or scripted beats combine axes so outcomes can be **noncombat-first** (shakedown, audit, rumor clash, parade obstruction) before violence unlocks.

## Contradictory rumor traffic

Districts may surface **incompatible social truths** simultaneously — encounter text should cite **which channel** lied (`architecture/clue-artifacts-rumor-packets.md`).

## Authority interruptions

Encounters can branch into **police sweep**, **faction pickup**, or **sponsor recall** based on clocks and legitimacy.

## Integration

- **Case generation** — reuse district schedule weights where implemented.
- **SPE-86 service nodes** — clientele overlaps encounter draws.

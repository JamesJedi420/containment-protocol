# Expansion, Foothold Establishment, and Delayed Yield (SPE-67)

## Purpose

New **footholds** (sites, districts, contracts, or sponsored presences) produce **durable value only after** vulnerable setup completes. **Nominal control** (flag planted, deed signed) is **not** the same as productive yield.

## Staged foothold pipeline

Author footholds to move through explicit stages:

1. **Discovery** — opportunity visible; costs and risks partially unknown.
2. **Commitment** — resources locked; opportunity cost begins; rivals may react.
3. **Setup** — construction, staffing, doctrine transfer, or ritual anchoring in progress; **high disruption surface**.
4. **Consolidation** — defenses, legitimacy hooks, or supply lines catch up to footprint.
5. **Delayed yield** — revenue, intel, access, or capability bonuses turn on only when consolidation criteria pass.

Stages may **skip forward** only when deterministic shortcuts exist (sponsor turnkey package, inherited infrastructure).

## Failure modes before yield

- **Stall** — funding, legitimacy, or specialist shortage freezes setup without failing the contract.
- **Disruption** — raids, audits, faction sabotage, or anomaly bleed set back clocks or destroy partial work.
- **Abandonment** — player or sponsor withdraws; partial assets may convert to salvage, liability, or hostile reuse.

## Tradeoffs

- **Speed vs exposure** — fast setup increases signature, inspection risk, or neighbor hostility.
- **Thin vs heavy commitment** — light footprint is cheaper but easier to dislodge before consolidation.

## Integration

- **Hub / factions** — opportunity quality and rumor density shift with foothold stage.
- **Persistence** — partial foothold state must save; abandoning mid-setup must not corrupt dependent systems.

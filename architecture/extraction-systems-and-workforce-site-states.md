# Extraction systems and workforce site states

## Source

Synced from Linear Containment Protocol project resource (2026-05-12). Git is canonical for ongoing edits. Original: [Linear doc](https://linear.app/spectranoir/document/extraction-systems-and-workforce-site-states-3f63f82d08ff).

## Scope

Covers procedural extraction, deposit-yield state, workforce-status triggers, throughput implications, and site-state changes driven by labor, occupancy, and industrial activity.

## Included issue boundaries

- SPE-419
- SPE-420

## Procedural extraction and yield

- Extraction value should not come from one flat rarity roll.
- Separate procedural factors may include deposit chance, size, grade, vein direction, depth band, extraction throughput, raw yield, concentrated yield, and refined yield.
- Labor capability, manual skill, rank, and deposit quality can all affect realized value.
- Concentration or preprocessing may reduce carrying weight at the cost of loss, with different ratios for ore-like versus gem-like outputs.
- Raw ore, concentrated cargo, and processed output remain distinct handling states.

## Workforce-state triggers

- Layoffs, furloughs, dismissals, or labor-status shifts can act as authored site-state switches rather than mere flavor.
- Workforce-state changes may alter who remains on site, who becomes resentful, and how vulnerable a facility becomes to intrusion, rumor leakage, or ownership instability.
- Staffing change is not the same as generic morale drift; it directly affects occupancy, local knowledge, and security weakness.

## Distinctions to preserve

- Deposit generation is not the same as later refinement or valuation.
- Workforce-state triggers are not the same as personal morale.
- Industrial site state should stay deterministic and inspectable rather than dissolving into generic colony simulation.

## Non-goals

- No full industrial economy simulator.
- No full labor-relations or HR simulator.
- No assumption that visible staffing change is purely cosmetic.

## Expected use

Use this doc when a site’s extraction potential, realized output, occupancy, vulnerability, or rumor profile depends on procedural deposit state and workforce status rather than a static site template.

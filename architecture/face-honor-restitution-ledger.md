# Face, Honor, and Restitution Ledger (SPE-171)

## Purpose

Social standing (“face,” honor) is tracked **separately** from money, rank, and coercive force. Etiquette outcomes, insult pressure, apology, gift, penance, and outsider/insider asymmetry are **first-class social mechanics**, especially before violence.

## Ledger model

Track:

- **Face balance** — current standing for individuals and groups.
- **Events** — insults, snubs, hospitality failures, violations, reconciliations.
- **Restitution actions** — apology, gift, penance, service, public acts.

## Outsider vs insider

Outsiders and insiders operate on **different baselines**:

- outsiders start with lower expected knowledge and can offend more easily,
- insider breaches may be more serious but also easier to repair through established channels.

## Feud and prewar escalation

Face failures can escalate into:

- feuds,
- alliance strain,
- or prewar conditions —

before crossing into open conflict.

## Integration

- **Obligation / patronage (SPE-51)** — many obligations are expressed in face and debt.
- **Legitimacy and factions** — certain factions may care primarily about honor outcomes.

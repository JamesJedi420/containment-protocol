# Frenzy, Resolve Shock, and Berserk Specialist Lane (SPE-84)

## Purpose

This lane models **unstable high-output states** — deliberate risk trades, not a general-purpose damage steroid. It is an **identity / doctrine** lane with aftermath debt.

## Entry and buildup

- **Battlefield-triggered buildup** — exposure to specific tags (blood, psi noise, certain anomaly classes) advances a bounded meter.
- **Self-harm or induction rituals** — authored costs (HP, integrity, equipment) to skip buildup or guarantee entry under pressure.
- **Resolve shock** — sudden threshold crossings from ally loss, betrayal, or failed containment that can force entry without consent.

## In-state behavior

- **Forced target fixation** — reduced target set or compulsive pursuit of tagged enemies; may ignore softer objectives.
- **Partial control loss** — player or AI policy tables choose between risky actions under narrowed legal bands.
- **High output** — damage, speed, or anomaly channel amplification with **explicit caps** per variant.

## Exit and debt

- **Collapse** — deterministic crash into exhaustion, injury spike, or unconsciousness.
- **Recovery debt** — multi-week readiness penalties, trauma tags, or medical ladder load (`architecture/medical-stabilization-response.md`).

## Anti-patterns

- Generic “rage buff” with no control degradation or downstream cost.
- Unlimited sustain without clocks or integrity coupling where authored.

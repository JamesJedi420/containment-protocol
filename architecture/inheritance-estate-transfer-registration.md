# Inheritance, Registration, and Estate Transfer (SPE-148)

## Purpose

Succession is a **legal and civic process**, not silent property teleportation. Estate transfer depends on registration, taxes, monopoly overlays, and invalidation rules.

## Core flow (conceptual)

1. **Claim**: heir, executor, or sponsor asserts succession.
2. **Registration**: the claim is recorded with a jurisdictional authority (may require fees, witnesses, or credential proofs).
3. **Validation**: will authenticity, identity, and conflict checks; compromised or unrecognized instruments can be invalidated.
4. **Overlay application**: taxes, monopoly rules, debt liens, sanctions, or curfew-era exceptions modify what can be transferred.
5. **Control transfer**: assets move into lawful control, with continuing obligations attached.

## Invalidation conditions

Invalidation is deterministic from explicit flags:

- forged or unrecognized wills,
- identity mismatch / possession contamination (SPE-126),
- emergency governance overrides (SPE-147),
- corruption or seizure actions.

## Outputs

Transfers should surface:

- new controller,
- obligations (tax, service, reporting),
- access keys or credential changes (SPE-137),
- public vs hidden ownership splits when illicit assets exist (SPE-86).

## Integration

- **Civic governance (SPE-87)**: courts, permit offices, and registries are mechanical nodes.
- **Supply and logistics**: estate assets can include transport rights or node control (SPE-72).

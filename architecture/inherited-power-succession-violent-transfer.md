# Inherited Power, Succession, and Violent Transfer (SPE-166)

## Purpose

Lineage-backed authority is a **distinct progression layer** with deterministic **no-gain / new-gain / upgrade** outcomes, recognized succession paths, and **violent extraction**. It is not static bloodline flavor or generic loot transfer.

## State surfaces

Maintain separate surfaces for:

- **Latent lineage potential** — who could inherit in principle.
- **Realized inheritance** — who currently carries lineage-backed authority.
- **Transferred authority** — which roles, powers, or assets have actually moved.

## Outcomes

Succession attempts and defeats can result in:

- **No gain** — lineage potential remains unclaimed or blocked.
- **New gain** — authority granted where none existed before.
- **Upgrade** — existing role raised in rank or scope.

## Recognized succession paths

Paths must be documented:

- legal / civic (courts, registries, religious law),
- doctrinal (orders, churches, cults),
- occult or anomaly-driven (anchors, geases).

Each path has conditions and fail states.

## Violent extraction

Defeated exceptional actors can have lineage power:

- **seized** (usable),
- **broken** (lost to world),
- or **re-routed** (to sponsor, faction, or apex).

Transfer history should be **inspectable** for accountability and narrative continuity.

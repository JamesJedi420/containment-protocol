# Institution Records, Calendars, and Affiliated Orders (SPE-174)

## Purpose

Institutions — religions, orders, cults, schools, guilds, civic bodies — use **repeatable operational records** with doctrine, calendars, permissions, major centers, affiliated orders, branch divergence, and maintenance metadata. They are not just prose blocks or single-voice faith notes.

## Institution schema (conceptual)

Key fields:

- **Doctrine** — core beliefs, taboos, and mission statements.
- **Calendars** — public performance schedules, festivals, fasts, trial days.
- **Permissions** — who may do what, where, with what training or clearance.
- **Centers** — major sites and their functions.
- **Affiliated orders** — child branches with specializations.
- **Branch divergence** — where children differ from parent on doctrine or practice.
- **Maintenance metadata** — last reformation, schism events, audit history.

## Hidden vs public structure

- **Public face** — visible rituals, architecture, professed doctrine.
- **Hidden shrines / cult infrastructure** — secret chapels, black archives, covert training sites.

Track both surfaces and route state transitions deterministically.

## Institutional memory

Institutions keep:

- records,
- registries,
- correspondence,

that allow them to remember prior decisions, betrayals, and favors beyond individual lifespans.

## Integration

- **Legitimacy and factions** — institutions are faction-like actors with special structure.
- **Calendars** affect district cadence and opportunity timing.

# Integrity Drift, Corruption, and Agency Loss Thresholds (SPE-79)

## Purpose

**Integrity** is an **active gameplay metric** — moral, psychological, civic, or legitimacy-framed depending on **campaign authoring** — not optional morality flavor. It **bounds** what methods remain institutionally sustainable.

## Central metric behavior

- **Bounded scale** — clamped bands with explicit low/critical/catastrophic thresholds.
- **Framing variance** — same mechanical spine can read as “moral injury,” “civic trust,” or “sponsor confidence” per campaign; document the skin, not only the number.
- **Use-alone erosion** — certain **dangerous methods** reduce integrity **on successful use**, not only on failure (torture-adjacent ops, forbidden bindings, mass deception against civilians).

## Divergence from competence

Integrity drift can **diverge from competence growth**: highly capable agencies may still become **illegitimate or ungovernable**. Track separately from skill or research tiers.

## Recovery paths

- **Renunciation** — explicit policy change, dismantling of programs, or public accountability beats that cost capability.
- **Reparative action** — funded restitution, tribunal compliance, or multi-week repair arcs that consume slots and cash.

Recovery is **slow and inspectable** — not a menu purchase.

## Agency-loss thresholds

At deterministic catastrophic thresholds, **control status** may change: receivership, hostile oversight, sponsor seizure of command, or game-ending institutional collapse — per authored campaign.

## Integration

- **Legitimacy / factions** — integrity couples to access and contract quality.
- **SPE-80 bound entities** — risky procedures accelerate drift when loopholes fire.

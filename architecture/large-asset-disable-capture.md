# Large Asset Damage, Disable, and Capture (SPE-65)

## Purpose

**Large assets and installations** — transports, support craft, carriers, heavy platforms, fixed sites — use a **distinct interaction grammar** from human-scale HP. Outcomes include **degrade, disable, capture, occupy, tow, crash, overcapacity stress, and repurposing**, not only destruction.

## Subsystem degradation

Resolve major damage through **compact subsystem loss** (mobility, sensors, power distribution, crew stations, ritual anchors) rather than a single hull number. Partial degradation changes **capabilities** and **strike options** next tick.

## Terminal branches (examples)

- **Disable** — immobilized or inert but repairable; may block routes or become cover.
- **Capture** — change of ownership with crew disposition (POW, evacuated, integrated).
- **Occupy** — friendly or hostile control of a stationary installation without full destruction.
- **Tow / salvage** — mobile asset recovery; consumes capacity and time.
- **Crash / catastrophic loss** — terminal branch with explicit footprint (debris field, hazard plume).
- **Overcapacity stress** — pushing systems (power, containment, population) past rated load without immediate breach; clocks advance.

Strike **success and effect** depend on **context and targeting conditions** (range band, escort presence, air superiority, EM environment, doctrine restrictions), not a single damage table.

## Cross-domain superiority

- **Escort / interception** — screens, CAP, or counter-battery modify whether a strike package reaches the asset.
- **Superiority toggles** — localized air, sea, subsurface, or liminal dominance gates certain branches.

## Downstream capability

Captured or lightly damaged assets may **transfer capabilities** (fire control, lift capacity, intel gear) into future weeks; degraded friendly assets **remove** capabilities until repaired. Track provenance for legitimacy and research.

## Integration

- **Mission resolution** — references to large-asset strike/capture and platform operations should consume this grammar.
- **Economy / procurement** — salvage and repurposing tie to custody and channels.

## Anti-patterns

- Collapsing all platform outcomes into “HP to zero = explode.”
- Ignoring crew, cargo, and hazardous materials when capture branches fire.

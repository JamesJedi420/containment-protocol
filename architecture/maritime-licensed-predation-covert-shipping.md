# Maritime Control, Licensed Predation, and Covert Shipping (SPE-140)

## Purpose

**Sea movement** is shaped by **license state**, **selective protection**, **prey tagging**, **covert reporting chains**, and **route-class secrecy** — not neutral transport with random piracy.

## License and racket surfaces

- **Licensed passage** — pay tolls to powers that may also sell **safe-conduct forgeries**.
- **Selective interdiction** — factions stop only tagged cargoes or flagged hulls.
- **Prey-state tagging** — vessels marked as lawful prize, smuggler, refugee, or military aux.

## Economics of predation

- **Controlled resale** of seized cargo feeds faction clocks and legitimacy (`systems/factions-legitimacy.md`).
- **Covert reporting** — coast watchers file dual reports (public vs sponsor channel) with different truth bits.

## Route geometry

- **Chokepoints** — straits, canals, river mouths displace routes onto ambush-heavy classes.
- **Tide-gated concealment** — low water exposes smuggler cuts; high water hides reefs.
- **Monster-run surveillance** — non-human patrols alter **secrecy and ambush profiles** per route class.

## Route classes

Each class (coastal cabotage, blue-water, riverine, liminal rift-sea) declares **baseline encounter odds**, **visibility**, and **escort doctrine**.

## Integration

- **SPE-72 supply network** — ports and ferries are strategic nodes.
- **SPE-90 pursuit** — boarding and interception tie to maritime contests.

# Maritime Logistics and Staged Naval Action (SPE-173)

## Purpose

Sea power is a **first-class campaign element**, integrating fleets, ports, convoying, blockade, sea-route logistics, staged naval engagements, and maritime horror pressures with the larger simulation — not an edge-case extension of land movement.

## Fleets, ports, and bases

- **Fleets** — groups of platforms (SPE-92) with doctrine, supply, and repair needs.
- **Ports** — strategic nodes (SPE-72) for repair, trade, and embarkation.
- **Migratory support bases** — seasonal or mobile hubs (icebreakers, platforms).

## Staged naval sequences

Naval action runs through compact phases:

- detection and approach,
- long-range exchange,
- close engagement / boarding,
- aftermath (salvage, tow, scuttle, occupation).

Each phase has integration points for horror, anomaly, or logistics outcomes.

## Route-filter hazard volumes

Certain sea lanes accumulate:

- derelicts,
- minefields,
- anomaly zones,

creating **hazard volumes** that affect logistics and encounter generation.

## Blockade and convoy

- **Blockade** — pressure on ports and trade; legitimacy and war state gates.
- **Convoying** — reduces risk at cost of speed and concentration vulnerabilities.

## Integration

- **Maritime control (SPE-140)** — predation and licensing overlay this structure.
- **Supply network (SPE-72)** — naval logistics determine throughput.

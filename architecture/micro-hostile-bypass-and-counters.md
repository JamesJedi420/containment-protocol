# Micro-hostile bypass and counters

## Source

Synced from Linear Containment Protocol project resource (2026-05-12). Git is canonical for ongoing edits. Original: [Linear doc](https://linear.app/spectranoir/document/micro-hostile-bypass-and-counters-6e3cb6056578).

## Scope

Covers nuisance-scale hostile entities and other very small attackers whose threat profile depends on bypassing ordinary protective assumptions rather than overpowering targets directly.

## Included issue boundary

- SPE-381

## Core rules

- Tiny or nuisance hostiles may ignore ordinary clothing or armor layers when their attack vector plausibly bypasses broad protective coverage.
- Their first value is often disruption rather than raw damage. Valid first-order effects include panic, laughter, noise, distraction, precision loss, and similar disable states before attrition begins.
- These entities should preserve sharp counters from specific materials or elements such as fire, water, or other authored environmental weaknesses.
- Counter-material interaction must change behavior materially rather than only adding flat damage.

## Distinctions to preserve

- Armor bypass is not universal anti-armor logic. It belongs to specific nuisance-hostile classes.
- Disable-first behavior is distinct from conventional swarm damage races.
- Counter-material logic is distinct from generic vulnerability tags; it should produce a recognizable procedural response.

## Non-goals

- No universal tiny-entity simulator.
- No assumption that armor always mediates all hostile contact.
- No sprawling insect-logic subsystem.

## Expected use

Use this doc when authoring small hostile entities that derive pressure from getting through ordinary protection, disrupting victims quickly, and being sharply answerable by the right material or environmental response.

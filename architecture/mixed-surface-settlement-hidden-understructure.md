# Hidden Civic Understructure in Mixed-Surface Settlements (SPE-154)

## Purpose

Settlements can preserve a coherent **public identity** while concealing hostile underlayers, covert institutions, hidden routes, and civic-abduction pipelines. Authoring must preserve the hard split between **visible civic shell** and **operational truth**.

## Two-pass authoring

1. **Ordinary function first**: markets, schools, clinics, permits, worship, transport.
2. **Hidden function second**: undercity ecologies, soft containment districts, covert logistics, false comfort towns.

This prevents “everything is secretly evil” incoherence and keeps public behavior legible.

## Understructure patterns (examples)

- **Concealed routes**: service tunnels, ritual corridors, sewer cuts.
- **Covert institutions**: black clinics, detention basements, sponsor labs (SPE-86).
- **Adaptive architecture**: rooms and streets that re-route under clocks (bounded).
- **Soft containment**: districts that trap or dampen anomalies while appearing normal.
- **Civic abduction pipelines**: permits, shelters, or clinics that funnel targets.
- **False-comfort towns**: friendly staging that is actually a capture net.

## Shell vs truth records

Maintain:

- **public shell** data for player navigation,
- **truth map** for simulation and hidden-state interactions,
- **reveal clauses** that determine how and when the split collapses.

## Integration

- **SPE-147 emergency governance**: discovery can trigger crackdown overlays.
- **SPE-144 settlements**: polity-driven generation can seed understructure likelihood.

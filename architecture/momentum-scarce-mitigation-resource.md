# Momentum — Spend, Refill, and Invalidation (SPE-89)

## Purpose

**Momentum** is a **scarce, bounded meta-resource** for **major actors only** (campaign-scale principals or authored “hero” slots — define per mode). It exists for **swing mitigation** and **continuity preservation**, not as generic hero points or free narrative rewrites.

## Allowed uses

- Reduce severity of a **single** outcome band shift when rules permit (e.g., partial instead of fail).
- Preserve **costly continuity** (key relationship, critical evidence chain) without nullifying the underlying failure.

Momentum **does not** erase consequence: follow-on clocks, legitimacy, or integrity costs may still apply.

## Depletion and refill

- **Explicit spend** — each use decrements a counter or consumes a token bucket; log reason codes.
- **Refill** — only via authored arcs: rest chapters, sponsor boons, training milestones, or crisis resolution — not passive regeneration every week by default.
- **Reset** — chapter breaks or mode switches may zero momentum with summary surfacing.

## Phase-based invalidation

Momentum may be **voided** when:

- entering specific mission phases (e.g., tribunal, public hearing),
- integrity or legitimacy falls below thresholds,
- or anti-momentum tags apply ( authored “no heroic saves” zones).

Invalidation must be **deterministic** and **surfaced** before spend is attempted.

## Anti-patterns

- Unlimited spend pools tied to narration beats.
- Using momentum to bypass weakest-link or integrity rules without explicit exception contracts.

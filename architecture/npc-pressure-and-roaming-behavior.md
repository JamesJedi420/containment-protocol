## Scope

Covers explicit behavioral pressure metadata, vendetta and claim pursuit, persistent search roaming, and related NPC state surfaces that should drive encounter posture and placement without requiring full personality simulation.

## Behavioral pressure metadata

- NPCs may carry explicit authored pressure fields such as phobias, nemeses, inheritance conflict, morale fragility, guilt, fear triggers, unstable prophecy filters, or similar behavioral drivers.
- These are not just prose notes; they are structured metadata that can affect leverage, posture, escalation, clue output, and social credibility.
- Pressure metadata should stay compact and inspectable rather than dissolving into generic biography.

## Unstable adviser channels

- Some high-value advisers remain useful precisely because their output is filtered through prophecy, trauma, visions, inherited memory, or altered perception.
- Instability-filtered insight is distinct from simple lying or random noise.
- Such advisers may shape expectation, narrow search space, or provide partial confirmations without becoming clean oracle pipes.
- Low-status diviners or unstable advisers may seed short-lived prophecy hooks that materially alter later interpretation even when prediction accuracy is imperfect.

## Vendetta and claim pursuit

- Some NPCs are driven by long-horizon vendetta, dispossession, inheritance recovery, or claim loops rather than static location loyalty.
- These pursuit states can change alliances, likely encounter zones, target priority, and escalation paths over time.
- Important NPCs should not be assumed homebound if their primary motive is pursuit.

## Persistent search roaming

- NPCs may roam according to active searches for people, objects, missing ties, or unresolved derivative counts rather than random wandering or fixed patrol loops.
- Search state can shape likely placement, rumor paths, and encounter corridors.
- Summoned or conjured search agents may share the same target objective while preserving provenance about who sent them and why.
- Guardian or allied ecologies may relay search or response behavior across wider terrain rather than only acting at direct contact range.

## Distinctions to preserve

- Pressure metadata is distinct from generic background notes.
- Vendetta pursuit is distinct from ordinary faction hostility.
- Search roaming is distinct from migration or looped patrol behavior.
- Unstable advisership is distinct from full oracle simulation.

## Non-goals

- No full personal-drama simulator.
- No full sandbox pursuit AI.
- No assumption that all mobile NPCs use one roaming model.
- No full oracle sandbox.

## Expected use

Use this doc when an NPC’s likely placement, long-horizon behavior, leverage profile, or encounter tone should be driven by explicit authored pressures, unstable insight channels, or search goals rather than only by site ownership or static faction role.

# Objective degradation and capture doctrine

## Source

Synced from Linear Containment Protocol project resource (2026-05-12). Git is canonical for ongoing edits. Original: [Linear doc](https://linear.app/spectranoir/document/objective-degradation-and-capture-doctrine-fff69da04e52).

## Scope

Covers mission targets that degrade over time and hostile doctrines that prioritize capture, detention, repurposing, or ritual use over immediate kill.

## Included issue boundaries

- SPE-424
- SPE-425

## Progressive objective-damage clocks

- Recoverable objectives can degrade through several stages before full loss.
- Timed deterioration may occur while the target remains in enemy custody or hostile conditions.
- Travel duration, delay, or route choice should materially change the recovered outcome quality.
- This is distinct from an instant fail state or a static objective.

## Capture-priority hostile behavior

- Some hostile groups prioritize living captives because prisoners have ritual, labor, identity, memory, leverage, feeding, or staging value.
- Capture logic may switch by target state: armed or actively resisting targets may be killed, while unarmed, weakened, or high-value targets are captured.
- Nonlethal defeat can route into stripping, tying, bait placement, imprisonment, conversion prep, slave-force reassignment, laboratory roles, or source-entity delivery.
- Downed operators do not automatically leave play in hostile territory when capture is the intended outcome.

## Nested captive states

- Captives may remain alive as renewable resources, ritual inputs, coerced specialists, bait, or compromised rescue risks.
- Prisoner states may vary across collaborators, frightened nonactors, resigned captives, escape planners, sedated subjects, partially converted subjects, and deliberately preserved specimens.
- Rescue should treat captives as potentially compromised rather than automatically safe on retrieval.

## Distinctions to preserve

- Progressive objective loss is distinct from target death.
- Capture priority is distinct from generic nonlethal branches.
- Live captives are operational state, not passive flavor.

## Non-goals

- No universal prison simulator.
- No universal slave-raid simulator.
- No assumption that all hostiles optimize for immediate kills.

## Expected use

Use this doc when a mission’s stakes depend on timed target degradation, live-capture logic, captive downstream state, or the difference between kill-first and capture-first hostile behavior.

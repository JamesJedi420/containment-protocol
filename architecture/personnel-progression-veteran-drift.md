# Long-Horizon Personnel Progression and Veteran Drift (SPE-60)

## Purpose

Personnel change over a campaign in **uneven**, **partially irreversible** ways. This doc rejects **linear stat accumulation** and **fully reversible respec** as the default mental model for veterans.

## Lifecycle and drift

Operatives move through **lifecycle stages** (early career, prime, veteran, late career, restricted duty, etc.) with **non-uniform** capability mutation:

- **Retained judgment** — pattern recognition, doctrine fluency, and crisis heuristics may **improve or plateau** long after physical peak.
- **Legacy skill** — deep mastery in a narrow lane (equipment family, anomaly class, jurisdiction) persists as a **slow-decay** asset.
- **Declining acute responsiveness** — speed, sensory acuity, sustained-stress tolerance, or recovery elasticity may **degrade** on independent curves.

Drift is **vector-valued**, not one “level.”

## One-way retraining

Some **retraining paths are one-way**: completing them **preserves old competencies** (legacy flags remain) while **routing future growth** through a new role or clearance band.

Examples of design intent:

- Medic → analyst pivot keeps triage intuition but removes field deployment eligibility.
- Field lead → staff instructor retains command bonuses in training contexts only.

Authoring should mark which skills are **frozen legacy**, which **decay**, and which **advance** under the new path.

## Integration

- **Team management** — readiness, role coverage, and cohesion read veteran vectors.
- **Training (SPE-14)** — incumbent certification and refresher policy interact with legacy vs current role gates.
- **Mission resolution** — weakest-link may treat “high judgment / low reflex” as a distinct profile.

## Anti-patterns

- Single “level” that rises forever.
- Free respec that erases narrative and mechanical history without trace.

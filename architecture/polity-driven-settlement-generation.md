# Polity-Driven Settlement Generation (SPE-144)

## Purpose

**Settlements** derive from **polity**, **technology**, **economy**, **extraction pressure**, **mixed loyalties**, **edge terrain**, and **dominant civic premise** — not population count alone.

## Generation axes

- **Polity** — charter type, corruption band, religion or ideology stack.
- **Technology** — power grid, sanitation, comms depth.
- **Economy** — dominant industry, trade role, debt to outsiders.
- **Extraction pressure** — mines, fisheries, occult wells stressing ecology or labor.
- **Mixed loyalties** — factions, diasporas, occupation forces cohabiting.

## Terrain and siting

- **Edge-terrain forts** — ridges, river bends, sinkhole rims anchor defensive seeds (`architecture/spatial-layers-exposure.md`).

## Temporary and refugee origins

Camps may **graduate** to villages via authored thresholds (market persistence, legitimacy grant) or **dissolve** under pressure.

## Market-to-village emergence

Trade posts accrete **services → housing → governance** packages deterministically when thresholds pass.

## Civic profile

Each settlement exports a **compact premise sentence** (“company town on a dying aquifer”) for encounter and rumor weighting (SPE-139).

## Integration

- **SPE-109 district cadence** — urban cores inherit schedule templates from polity.
- **SPE-72** — ports and nodes anchor trade settlements.

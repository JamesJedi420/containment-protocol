# Procedural Geases and Bounded Wish Resolution (SPE-157)

## Purpose

Geases and bounded wishes are **modular obligation / request contracts**, not bespoke one-off story magic. They assemble from reusable components with **explicit task, object, creature, destination, and failure-consequence structures** and **backlash bands**.

## Obligation / wish packet shape

Core fields:

- **Task** — what must be done or avoided.
- **Object** — what must be delivered, protected, or destroyed.
- **Creature / target** — who the obligation centers on.
- **Destination / locus** — where it must occur.
- **Deadline / window** — when or under what conditions.
- **Failure consequences** — graded penalties with explicit caps.

## Coerced redemption service

Geases can encode **service as reprieve** (e.g., criminal diverted into hazardous missions). Obligations must be:

- bounded in time and scope,
- testable for completion,
- reversible in terms of legal or doctrinal status when fulfilled.

## Penalties and backlash

Penalties may be:

- **body-targeted** — injury, weakness, sensory loss,
- **function-targeted** — blocked abilities, tool incompatibility,
- **social / civic** — loss of status, contract invalidation,
- **metaphysical** — anchor drift (SPE-125), integrity loss (SPE-79).

Backlash bands should be capped and documented per geas or wish family.

## Integration

- **Sleeper conditioning (SPE-133)** — may coexist with or be enforced via geas constraints.
- **Integrity and conduct (SPE-146)** — vows can be backed by geas-like enforcement.

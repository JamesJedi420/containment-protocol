# Progressive Corruption and Managed Manifestation (SPE-169)

## Purpose

Dangerous power use creates **staged body and identity drift** with visible combat and social consequences, **partial manifestation options**, **hybrid override states**, and **irreversible thresholds**. Corruption from self-origin powers is distinct from possession.

## Staged drift

Corruption advances through stages tied to:

- use frequency,
- dependence (using power to solve everything),
- acquisition events.

Each stage alters:

- appearance,
- capabilities,
- social cues.

## Manifestation modes

- **Partial manifestation** — only some traits express (eyes, voice, limb).
- **Hybrid override** — power and self share control; behavior is neither pure self nor pure possession.
- **Full conversion** — rare terminal state with changed alignment of body and identity.

Irreversible thresholds should be declared; rolling them back requires explicit doctrine breaks or miracles.

## Emotional and symbolic triggers

Triggers such as humiliation, victory, loss, or symbolic acts can spike corruption stages; these are explicit escalation paths.

## Distinction from possession

Corruption here is **self-origin** drift — possession is covered by identity overwrite (SPE-126) and may coexist but remains separate.

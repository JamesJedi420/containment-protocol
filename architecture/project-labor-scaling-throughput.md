# Project Labor Scaling and Throughput (SPE-150)

## Purpose

Build and excavation projects (fortifications, tunnels, traps, siegeworks, digs) consume explicit **labor**, **time**, **support capacity**, and **substrate-sensitive effort**. Progress is not “generic % per week.”

## Labor-weeks vs calendar-weeks

Distinguish:

- **Labor-weeks**: total work units required.
- **Calendar-weeks**: time elapsed on the campaign clock.

Worker scaling converts labor-weeks to calendar-weeks with diminishing returns, interference, and logistics constraints.

## Throughput formula ingredients (conceptual)

- **Crew count** and role mix
- **Substrate**: rock, mud, ruins, cursed lattice
- **Support dependencies**: engineers, maintenance, med support (SPE-94)
- **Logistics**: deliveries and access (SPE-72)
- **Method**: careful, rushed, covert, coercive

## Coercive labor degradation

Coercion can increase short-term labor but degrades:

- quality,
- safety,
- civic exposure and unrest (SPE-87),
- long-run throughput (strike, sabotage, desertion).

## Multipliers

Allow bounded multipliers:

- hero / spellcaster / specialist boosts (declared, capped),
- tool chains and siege engines,
- station services (SPE-141).

## Search throughput (work-method-sensitive)

Certain project methods change hidden search throughput (SPE-136):

- careful excavation reveals more,
- rushed digging collapses caches into degraded salvage states.

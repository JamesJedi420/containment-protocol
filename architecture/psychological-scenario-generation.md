# Psychological scenario generation

## Source

Synced from Linear Containment Protocol project resource (2026-05-12). Git is canonical for ongoing edits. Original: [Linear doc](https://linear.app/spectranoir/document/psychological-scenario-generation-e0cb4cecd252).

## Scope

Covers scenario and encounter payloads that derive from a target's biography, losses, guilt, fears, fractured self-image, future anxieties, and parallel viewpoint tracks rather than generic fear imagery.

## Included issue boundary

- SPE-433

## Core rules

- Psychological encounter chains may be generated from remembered events, betrayals, moral guilt, feared futures, self-fracture, and comparable biographical source categories.
- The same encounter framework should produce different content when the target biography changes.
- Biography-driven payloads are distinct from generic hallucination, generic fear, or generic dream-state content.

## Parallel protagonist and viewpoint tracks

- A scenario may advance through two or more concurrent viewpoint tracks that later converge rather than one uninterrupted player-thread only.
- Parallel tracks should remain bounded, inspectable, and tied to authored scenario logic rather than becoming freeform narrative generation.

## Distinctions to preserve

- A psychological scenario is not just flavor text over a fixed encounter.
- Biographical source categories are not interchangeable generic mood tags.
- Future-fear content is distinct from past-trauma content even when both route through the same framework.

## Non-goals

- No universal dream simulator.
- No sprawling psychotherapy or full narrative-generation system.
- No assumption that all psychological encounters share the same imagery.

## Expected use

Use this doc when an encounter or scenario should be generated from who the target is, what they fear, what they regret, and what parallel-track perspective the scenario is trying to surface.

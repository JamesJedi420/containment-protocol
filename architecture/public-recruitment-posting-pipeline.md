# Public Recruitment Posting — Response Pipeline (SPE-149)

## Purpose

Public recruitment posts are not generic volume multipliers. **Visibility, wording, cost, venue, and local conditions** shape who sees and answers the call — and therefore applicant motives, gear, pay expectations, reliability, and risk.

## Posting surfaces

Posts can be placed on public nodes with different audiences:

- parks, markets, transit hubs,
- temples and union halls,
- service nodes (SPE-86),
- district notice boards under crackdown overlays (SPE-147).

Each surface provides deterministic modifiers for:

- audience volume,
- audience tier distribution,
- hostility / informant risk.

## Wording and signaling

Wording tags affect:

- applicant **motive** (patriot, desperate, mercenary, zealot),
- expectation alignment (hazard pay, secrecy, legality),
- self-selection (disciplined vs reckless).

## Cost and legitimacy

Postings may consume:

- currency,
- favors,
- reputation/standing,
- or legal permits.

Improper postings can trigger civic attention (SPE-87) or legitimacy penalties.

## Pipeline integration

Posting responses feed into the broader hiring funnels (SPE-107):

post → applicant wave → screening → onboarding load.

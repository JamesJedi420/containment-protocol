# Recruitment Markets, Contract Quality, and Impressment (SPE-107)

## Purpose

**Force generation** flows through **staged markets and hiring funnels**, not a flat manpower pool or abstract “+N recruits.” Volume, **quality**, **motive**, **loyalty**, and **risk** vary by settlement, posting, wages, reputation, coercion, and vetting depth.

## Market and funnel layers

- **Local markets** — each jurisdiction or hub district exposes a different applicant mix and wage pressure.
- **Applicant waves** — deterministic arrivals keyed to week, unrest, faction posture, and campaign clocks.
- **Hiring funnels** — screening → offer → onboarding → probation; each stage can drop or downgrade candidates.

## Acquisition channels

- **Public postings** — high volume, mixed quality, visible to rivals and press.
- **One-on-one hires** — lower volume, higher vetting cost, better motive/loyalty signal when successful.
- **Mercenary affiliation** — contract quality and desertion risk tied to sponsor and pay cadence.
- **Impressment / coercion** — fills slots fast with **loyalty and legitimacy debt**; triggers impressment fallout clocks and SPE-87 civic pressure when discovered.

## Outcome dimensions

Track separately (bounded enums or scores):

- **Quantity** — headcount offered this week.
- **Quality** — skill bands, health, experience.
- **Motive** — economic desperation, ideology, revenge, blackmail leverage.
- **Loyalty** — baseline and drift under treatment.
- **Risk** — infiltration, informant, medical liability, political exposure.

## Onboarding burden

Accepted recruits consume **training capacity**, **gear**, and **supervision** before they count as deployable; overload creates backlog pressure (`systems/team-management.md`).

## Labor-market pressure

Regional shortages, rival hiring, or casualty spikes shift wages and **applicant quality bands** without silent infinite supply.

## Integration

- **Hub / factions** — rumor and contract surfaces reflect market tightness.
- **SPE-56 compliance** — coerced recruits raise refusal and concealment risk.

# Sleeper Conditioning and Triggered Obedience (SPE-133)

## Purpose

**Ordinary actors** may carry **delayed trigger scripts** — not total puppet control. Obedience is **narrow**, **constraint-based**, and **counterplayable**.

## Trigger keys

Author deterministic activators:

- **symbols**, **tokens**, **phrases**, audio tones,
- **bounded state** (sleep depth, drug window, ritual phase),
- **dream / suggestion channels** (SPE-588 family when implemented).

## Obedience shape

Scripts should specify:

- **allowed action set** (e.g., “unlock this door,” “deliver this phrase,” “freeze 10s”),
- **forbidden actions** (cannot self-harm unless separate hazard),
- **duration** and **cooldown**,
- **awareness level** — does the actor remember obeying?

## Counterplay

- **Trigger denial** — remove token, block signal, sedate during window.
- **Discovery** — therapy, interrogation, or forensic ritual reveals script metadata without firing it.
- **Removal / burn-in** — costly procedures that delete or replace the script with integrity consequences.

## Integration

- **SPE-126** — conditioning can mimic possession symptoms; keep tracks separate.
- **SPE-131** — unconscious conditioned actors still occupy site state.

# Specialist Outsourcing, Transcription, and Flawed Output Risk (SPE-85)

## Purpose

Rare specialists may perform **core knowledge work** (transcription, copying, analysis, reproduction of sensitive assets) **on behalf of the agency** — but **contractor labeling does not remove agency responsibility** when core functions are effectively outsourced.

## Hidden quality bands

External deliverables carry **latent quality tiers** not fully visible at handoff: near-perfect, usable-with-errors, subtly wrong, or hostile-tainted. Discovery may require **delayed verification** tasks.

## Staged task flow

- **Partial results** — incremental drops that unblock some downstream work while hiding defects.
- **Compatibility constraints** — format, clearance, or ritual compatibility failures that surface late.

## Flawed persistent artifacts

Mistakes can **write bad state** into casefiles, templates, or training materials — persisting across weeks until a correction arc runs.

## Institutional risks

- **Turnover risk** — specialist departure mid-task leaves dangling references or poisoned half-finished assets.
- **Secrecy exposure** — broader readership for outsourcing increases leak surface.
- **Institutional memory loss** — over-reliance erodes in-house competence, raising **dependency** and **liability** when vendors fail or misrepresent work.

## Agency liability

Regulators and sponsors still attribute outcomes to the **agency** even when vendors signed waivers; model legitimacy and integrity consequences (`architecture/integrity-drift-corruption-agency-loss.md`, `systems/factions-legitimacy.md`).

## Anti-patterns

- “Hire vendor → perfect copy” shortcuts without verification queues.

# Submerged Site Interactions (SPE-163)

## Purpose

Underwater and sea-floor spaces are distinct **site classes** with their own **access, salvage, evidence, and movement assumptions** — not a single generic “underwater” tag.

## Site classes

- **Underwater / drowned** — partially submerged structures, variable depth.
- **Sea-floor** — anchored structures or wreckage on the bottom.
- **Hydrological anchors** — springs, vents, rifts that connect to other layers.
- **Wrecked vehicles / ships** — large-asset branches (SPE-65) in submerged state.

## Assumptions per class

Per class, specify:

- **access** — gear, vehicles, magic, or infrastructure required.
- **breathability** — air pockets vs mixed atmospheres vs full immersion.
- **movement** — drag, zero-g analogies, 3D pathing simplifications.
- **evidence** — decay, buoyancy, obscuration rules.

## Moisture and cave interactions

Active cave moisture and hydrological behavior tie into **subterranean topology** (SPE-111) and **regional hydrology** in persistence.

## Integration

- **Pursuit / platforms (SPE-90, SPE-92)** — some chases and platforms extend into submerged sites.
- **Supply network (SPE-72)** — underwater routes and ports act as special nodes.

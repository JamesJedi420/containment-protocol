# Summon taxonomy and control

## Source

Synced from Linear Containment Protocol project resource (2026-05-12). Git is canonical for ongoing edits. Original: [Linear doc](https://linear.app/spectranoir/document/summon-taxonomy-and-control-3ab079653272).

## Scope

Covers bounded summon families, control states, substrate constraints, and summon-runtime behavior where manifestation, obedience, legality, and environmental filtering are all authored explicitly.

## Included issue boundary

- SPE-405

## Family structure

- Summons do not all scale through one free parameter.
- Some families should use explicit tiered ladders with numbered or named progression.
- Other families should remain special-case structures such as familiars, stalkers, swarms, elementals, proxy hands, temporary servants, or battlefield-only formations.
- Summon outputs may be budgeted by total hits, levels, or aggregate capacity rather than one named unit type only.

## Substrate and environment filtering

- Some summons must draw from local substrate or source material.
- Local-substrate summons may succeed while still producing temperament mismatch, future vendetta, or lingering service liability.
- Some environments may intercept, substitute, or distort the intended summon result rather than honoring the clean output.
- Summon legality must remain medium- and environment-aware so aquatic, planar, sealed, or hostile contexts reject, substitute, or degrade results appropriately.

## Control and obedience

- Summoning is separate from control.
- Command loss, concentration break, hostile context, or interrupted preparation may shift a summon into uncontrolled or hostile behavior.
- Some summoned entities ignore ordinary morale behavior and obey simplified control states.
- Fidelity bands should distinguish crude motion, precise shaping, and high-control paired operation as separate authored states.
- Created ownership may transfer under hostile control, moving command, upkeep, and dismissal rights to a new controller.

## Runtime actor assumptions

- Summoned or created entities are real runtime actors with explicit capability omissions, delayed activation, upkeep source, dismissal authority, and creator-loss collapse differences.
- Some summons may occupy body channels, suppress speech, consume captive power sources, or leave unstable anchored residue even when the summon attempt fails.
- Powerful entities may resist or distort summoning if they interpret the destination as confinement, trap-world entrapment, or another hostile context.

## Distinctions to preserve

- Summoning is not the same as clean ally creation.
- Environment legality is not cosmetic flavor.
- Substrate dependence is not generic ammo cost.
- Tiered ladders and special-case families should coexist without being flattened into one taxonomy.

## Non-goals

- No universal summon sandbox.
- No sprawling spell-tree simulator.
- No assumption that obedience and clean return semantics are defaults.

## Expected use

Use this doc when a summon family needs explicit tier structure, special-case runtime behavior, substrate constraints, control loss, environment filtering, or ownership-state logic that should stay deterministic and inspectable.

# Transformation Control, Upkeep, and Reversion (SPE-132)

## Purpose

**Transformed states** — self or proxy — are **sustained systems** with **upkeep ceilings**, **focus dependencies**, and **explicit reversion** — not freeform polymorph buffs.

## Upkeep and limits

- **Upkeep ceilings** — maximum concurrent transformed hours per week or mana-like budgets keyed to campaign constants.
- **Focus-object dependencies** — amulets, fetishes, biotech collars; loss or jamming forces partial reversion or backlash.
- **Partial manifestations** — only limb, sense, or power subset active to save upkeep.

## Proxy maintenance

Third parties may **pay upkeep** for a transformed subject under authored contracts — creates leverage and betrayal surfaces.

## Retention rules

Author what persists across transform:

- **role / cognition** — memory, skills, clearance handling,
- **tool compatibility** — body plan may forbid certain gear tags,
- selective **capability loss** (speech, fine motor) even when gross strength rises.

## Reversion triggers

- **Controller disruption** — link severed, jammed, or hijacked.
- **Integrity or doctrine breach** — automatic snap-back when policy forbids current form.
- **Exhaustion of upkeep** — graceful degrade vs catastrophic snap per template.

## Anti-patterns

- Infinite sustain with no object or attention tether.
- Silent full heals bundled into transform without medical ladder interaction where relevant.

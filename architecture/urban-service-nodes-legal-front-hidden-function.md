# Urban Service Nodes — Legal Front and Hidden Function Layering (SPE-86)

## Purpose

**Urban service nodes** are **operational packets**, not generic vendor menus or shallow building props. Each node combines **public-facing service semantics** with optional **hidden functions** that affect intel, logistics, legitimacy, and pressure.

## Node packet (conceptual fields)

- **Operator identity** — owner, franchise, faction sponsor, or shell entity.
- **Service families** — what is openly offered (lodging, repair, clinic, courier, shrine, data bureau).
- **Availability** — hours, capacity, blackout windows, seasonal closure.
- **Clientele tags** — who is welcomed, tolerated, or refused (class, faction dress, clearance).
- **Rumor output** — deterministic rumor/leads the node feeds to hub simulation when conditions match.
- **Venue rules** — dress codes, weapons policies, recording bans, membership tiers.
- **Trust gates** — minimum standing, voucher, or introduction required for sensitive services.

## Legal front vs hidden function

- **Legal front** — audited, licensed surface behavior; may still be morally gray.
- **Hidden function layer** — optional contraband staging, informant handler role, **detention space**, covert logistics handoff, or black clinic annex. Hidden layers have **separate discovery and legitimacy** hooks.

## Temporary rented capabilities

Nodes may **lease** specialists, gear, or ritual capacity for bounded weeks — appears as tagged modifiers on the packet, not permanent roster growth.

## Node-level enforcement

Author explicit enforcement outcomes:

- **Confiscation** — gear or evidence seized under venue rules.
- **Expulsion** — forced exit with clock or reputational follow-up.
- **Social-class handling** — asymmetric service quality, pricing, or risk of shakedown.

## Integration

- **Hub** — opportunities and filtered signals consume rumor and trust outputs.
- **SPE-87 civic** — jurisdiction and permits gate node access or trigger raids.

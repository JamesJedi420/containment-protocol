# World-Law Compatibility and Contradiction Rules (SPE-151)

## Purpose

Domains expose explicit **support / suppression profiles** for capability families, item classes, transit methods, and ideological admission. Unsupported actions produce **contradiction states** with deterministic enforcement — not generic “it fails” behavior.

## World-law parameters (examples)

Treat these as first-class environment parameters:

- **Gravity / inertia bands**
- **Transit logic**: doors, thresholds, liminal travel, tunnels
- **Paradox tolerance**: what contradictions the domain can absorb
- **Ideology-tag admission**: who or what the place “accepts” (oathbound, sanctioned, profane)

## Support profiles

Each domain publishes a bounded profile:

- supported power families,
- suppressed families,
- conditional support (requires tokens, vows, or licenses),
- forbidden actions (illegal or metaphysically impossible).

## Contradiction states

When an unsupported action is attempted:

- record a **contradiction event** (who, where, what family),
- apply deterministic enforcement, for example:
  - backlash,
  - attention clocks,
  - partial effect with contamination,
  - forced reversion of transformations (SPE-132),
  - legality escalation (SPE-87 / SPE-147).

Contradictions persist until resolved or paid down; they are not erased by reload or re-roll.

## Integration

- **SPE-146 conduct gates**: vows may be required by domain admission.
- **SPE-70 hidden-state**: suppression may cause projection mismatches.
- **SPE-115 peril**: contradictions can advance survival gates.

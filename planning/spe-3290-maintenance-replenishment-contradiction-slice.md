## Approved decision — October 8, 2026

The user selected rejection of recurring free replenishment, an explicit resolution outcome inside SPE-3290. This records the existing contradiction-review boundary; it authorizes no new child or weekly-allocation implementation.

1. **Recurring free replenishment is prohibited.** Do not grant `maintenanceHours` or `partsReserve` merely because the campaign week advances. Explicit paid or staff/gasket replenishment remains allowed, including eligible conversions in successive weeks.
2. **Scarcity invariant:** after the fresh-campaign endowment, every resource increase requires either the existing canonical funding debit or the existing explicit staff-capacity commitment plus named-stock debit. Time passage, reload, migration, and replay cannot supply an additional free budget. Recovery consumes its canonical resource requirements; expansion continues to accrue maintenance debt independently.
3. **Grant policy:** no unconditional, conditional, capped, or campaign-state-dependent recurring free grant is allowed. This decision adds no balance caps, eligibility exceptions, or new numeric thresholds.
4. **Cadence authority:** use persisted `GameState.week`, not wall-clock time or a producer-local counter. SPE-3292 successful eligibility receipts are keyed by canonical staff ID and campaign week; advancing that week restores eligibility, not resources. `advanceWeek` passes `sourceState.week` to maintenance debt closeout, whose `lastProcessedWeek` prevents duplicate accrual. This uses the existing weekly authority referenced by SPE-1071 without claiming completion of its broader calendar scope.
5. **Resulting implementation boundary:** no recurring free weekly-allocation implementation proceeds. Preserve the three shipped sources below and existing recovery, persistence, and closeout contracts. No new child is needed. A future change to this rule requires separate scope governance.

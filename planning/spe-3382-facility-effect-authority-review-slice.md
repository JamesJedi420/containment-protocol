Use one synthetic bound biohazard facility with installed research slots `2`, speed `2`, training slots `2`, recovery throughput `3`; research base is slots/speed `1/1`. Valid transition history proves constructing/inspecting; upgrading has a coherent timer and unapplied `+1` pending slot. These are review fixtures, not new production authoring.

Current `FacilityStatus` does not include degraded, isolated, sealed, damaged, abandoned, destroyed, commandeered, or decommissioning. SPE-1564 body/comments own those future lifecycle contracts. `ready/degraded/unavailable` are already dependency-result states, not lifecycle aliases. Unsupported persisted status normalizes to inactive; do not author a future lifecycle value expecting implemented selective suppression. Future state-to-family policy and prerequisites remain unresolved until the owning inputs exist.

## Persistence and reproducible counterexamples

Installed effects remain intact through restriction/reopening (E2; `src/test/facilityLifecycle.test.ts:190`). Upgrade completion alone applies pending deltas and increments level; hydration keeps pending deltas only with coherent active timing under Done SPE-2549. Missing legacy history is not invented; malformed present history remains unavailable and blocks lifecycle commands. None of those rules turns stored capacity into effective capacity.

Executed against current modules using Vite SSR module loading, `createStartingState`, `stripGameTemplates` and `hydrateGame`; no tracked runtime/test files were changed:

1. For available, active, upgrading, inactive and locked, load the matrix fixture twice via `hydrateGame(stripGameTemplates(previous))`. Research slots/speed become `3/2`, then `5/4`; facility effects remain identical. This proves live repeated-load accumulation, not merely a hypothetical double-count. Existing one-load hydration coverage still passes and does not establish idempotence.
2. With installed speeds `1.5` and `2`, summary reports `3.5`, while direct research activation produces speed `3`. Reactivating its returned state produces speed `9` and slots `3 → 5`. A summary consumer must not treat a sum of multipliers as the current execution rate.
3. A locked level-2 facility satisfies a level-2 prerequisite in `recomputeResearchState`. Entirely absent facility state also leaves that project available; an explicitly empty facility registry blocks it. Level checks therefore represent historical structural qualification, not operational availability; absent input has a separate fail-open behavior.
4. `advanceResearchProgress` with speeds `1` and `3` advances time by one in both cases. No automatic pool output occurs from the named weekly-rate fields.
5. A locked facility with `trainingSlots: 2`, negative research slots and `customPassive: 7` hydrates to `{ researchSlots: 0, trainingSlots: 2 }`. Known installed capacity persists despite status; unknown key loss is existing allowlist policy, not new migration authority.

Legacy research scalars currently lack base-versus-applied provenance. Do not repair accumulation by guessing that every saved value is base capacity, subtracting a presumed bonus, zeroing installed effects on close, or adding a second effective-capacity ledger. SPE-3343 must determine bounded compatibility for research execution; a separate immediate hydration repair would require fresh reconciliation. Save/load validation must cover repeated cycles, upgrades before/after completion, absent facilities, stale/malformed lifecycle packets, and unchanged stored effects.

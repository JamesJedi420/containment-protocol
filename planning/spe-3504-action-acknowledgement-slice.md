# SPE-3504: bounded action acknowledgements

This slice projects maintenance conversion results and workshop queue/receipt evidence through the operational explanation record. A queued workshop order remains pending; a matching durable receipt records completion or degraded quality while a stale lane remains visible as awaiting reconciliation. An applied conversion reports only the resource conversion; replay and blocked requests do not claim a new effect. Receipt and transaction owners remain authoritative. Terminal success requires authoritative terminal evidence, not a request or queue entry.

The existing Agency and workshop surfaces retain their visibility boundaries. The selected producers do not supply all cancellation, supersession, no-effect, objective-confirmation or hidden-information cases required by SPE-3504; the issue and parent remain open. This slice does not introduce a new lifecycle or claim broader acceptance.

The protected remote-work Agency surface also projects its existing player-visible stage through the shared explanation record. It retains the canonical work ID, reports an aborted or failed operation without claiming objective success, and suppresses a transient announcement when the authoritative work stage changes. The procedure owner retains effect, exposure and lifecycle authority. Supersession and objective confirmation remain unimplemented here.
